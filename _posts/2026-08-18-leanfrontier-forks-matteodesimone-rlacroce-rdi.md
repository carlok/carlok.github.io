[carlok/LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) was forked by
[matteodesimone](https://github.com/matteodesimone/LeanFrontier) and
[rlacroce-rdi](https://github.com/rlacroce-rdi/LeanFrontier) on 2026-08-18. A
fork is this corpus's intake path rather than a copy: a submission arrives as a
branch on somebody else's fork and is judged there, so a new fork is the
outward sign that someone intends to try. Both used it that day — rlacroce-rdi
for the Josephus survivor closed form for step two
([PR #55](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/55), merged the same
afternoon) and Matteo De Simone for a run of submissions from the Fibonacci
Q-matrix to the Furstenberg topology on the integers
([PR #56](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/56) onward). The corpus is
at [carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/).
