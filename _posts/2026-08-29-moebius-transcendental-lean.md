[moebius-transcendental-lean](https://github.com/carlok/moebius-transcendental-lean)
is a new Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization, now public, of the conjugation degree
δ(z) = [Q̄(z, conj z) : Q̄(z)] on the transcendental locus ℂ ∖ Q̄, following the
companion paper p19.tex. It is archived with a
[Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22146649) and shipped as v0.1.0 and
v0.1.1 with CI and a permanent axiom-verification module. The δ = 1 stratum is
the subject of the companion [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean).
