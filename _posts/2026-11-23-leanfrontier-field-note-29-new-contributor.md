[Field Note 29](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/notes/field-note-29.html), *A New Contributor.*, is the first note since August about accepted work from anyone other than qazW12345: **Taermoon** opened three pull requests on 23 November and all three were merged that evening — the clique–coclique bound after Godsil and Meagher's Theorem 3.1 ([#558](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/558)), vertex deletion preserving connectivity of a connected graph of at least two vertices ([#559](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/559)), and the no-homomorphism lemma of Albertson and Collins, stated by cross-multiplying ([#560](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/560)) — each record naming OpenAI Codex running GPT-6, the model behind qazW12345's recent work but through a different agent. None of the three imports the corpus, so the series counts three new subjects and no new edges, and no bounded tactic closed any of them.

They are also the first corpus files that begin with `module` and use `public import` and `public section`, a form every reader in the project took for plain `import` and `section`: the receiver's probe context, the statement screen, the catalogue, the dataset and the accumulation series. Nothing was lost for these three, since they import only Mathlib and the probe imports all of it — but a later module-system file importing another corpus module would have been probed without that import and the series would have missed the edge, with no error anywhere. Every parser now reads both forms, and the same change fixed an older slip: `noncomputable section`, used by four corpus files, was not recognised, so its `end` was read as closing the namespace around it ([#565](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/565)).

The upgrade to Mathlib v4.36.0 took four attempts once it was dispatched by hand. `Nat.strong_induction_on` had renamed its case to `ind` and two proofs no longer built ([#561](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/561)); `leanchecker` segfaults on Linux whenever it is asked to check a module that has submodules, treating the name as a prefix and checking the whole family in one process, so the thirteen modules with submodules are now checked alone with `--fresh` — about two and a half minutes each, and a stronger check, since it replays every import ([#562](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/562)); the slower recheck stretched the job to 84 minutes and its one-hour token expired after the audit, so a fresh token is minted after it ([#563](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/563)). Then all 342 accepted entrypoints passed with no collision against Mathlib and no deprecation in the corpus, and the statement screen stated all 342; the corpus stands at 125 modules and 98 internal import edges. For v4.37 four proofs broke on its first release candidate and are already fixed on every version from v4.35 on ([#566](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/566)).
