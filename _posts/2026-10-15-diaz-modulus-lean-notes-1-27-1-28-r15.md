The Diaz companion note went out twice on 15 October. [v1.27](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.27) adds no result and corrects three attributions found by two blind referee reports: the step "a 2×3 configuration in a four-dimensional space is a geometric progression" inside Theorem 5.5 is Bachoc–Serra–Zémor (2017), Lemma 4; Fischler's Lemma 6.1 excludes four-term progressions from the logarithms of algebraic numbers with the six exponentials theorem, while Diaz's Théorème 7(2) is its form for ℒ̃, two passages having equated them; and the u³ limit holds for single powers through their hulls, where "nothing beyond" overstated it ([e3bb5c0](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/e3bb5c0), which also ranked chapter 14 tier 1 on a fixed rubric and credited proofs by `amorphic` and `curiyu`).

[v1.28](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.28) adds the day's four results to Proposition 5.12, the configurations whose factor has degree two. For `g = a₁/(u − a) + a₂/(u + a)` with `|a| ≠ |u|` and `a₁ ≠ a₂`, the space `span{1, u, u⁻¹, g, ḡ}` is conjugation-stable, spanned by the products of a configuration and invisible to Theorem B; the cyclic case `H₀ + Q̄G₁ + Q̄G₂`, with `v = u/s`, `G₁ = 1/(v − 1) + 2/(v + 1)` and `G₂ = 1/(v − 1)² + 1/(v − 1) − 2/(v + 1)² + 2/(v + 1)`, is five-dimensional, conjugation-stable, spanned by the products of `(1, u²)` and `(ū, G₁, G₂)`, invisible, and carries no configuration with a Möbius ratio, with `u²` acting on `G₁, G₂` as a Jordan block modulo `H₀`. Remark 5.13 now says each of the three degree-two families has a formal member, the mirror holds all 418 results after [R15](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/62c70ff) (four nodes, 27 files), the repository moved to Lean and Mathlib v4.35.0 the same day ([5a7871d](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/5a7871d)), and a TeX-only research note adds Theorem C — the conjugation hull of a point of a circle of algebraic radius carries a 2×3 configuration exactly when `deg P` is 2 or 3, so with Roy's strong six exponentials `e^{βP(u)}` is transcendental at a candidate for every quadratic and cubic `P` — and Theorem D, that adjoining a point of the circle generic over the field the logarithms generate preserves Baker, Hermite–Lindemann, Gel'fond–Schneider, six exponentials, Roy and four exponentials in transcendence degree one, yet still yields a candidate ([4ba0441](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/4ba0441)). The [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) mirror carries the same day from the research side: the R15 entry with milestones 134–136 ([3f4c701](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/3f4c701)) and the chapter's "what was not found" ranked, with the credits owed ([759d4e9](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/759d4e9)).
