[carlok/LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) was forked by
[giacomoleonzi](https://github.com/giacomoleonzi/LeanFrontier) on 2026-08-24. In
this corpus a fork is not a copy but the way in: a submission arrives as a branch
on somebody else's fork and is judged there, which is what
[Field Note 10](/blog/2026/09/01/leanfrontier-field-note-10/) was about. This
fork is the one the Tribonacci sequence submission later arrived through
([PR #167](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/167)). The corpus is at
[carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/).
