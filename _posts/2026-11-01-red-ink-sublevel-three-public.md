[red-ink](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink) went public on 1 November and plays at [carlok.github.io/red-ink](https://carlok.github.io/red-ink/): *Sublevel Three*, a browser fighting game where you audit pallet counts on the night shift at Halvorsen Freight until Pike hands you a keycard and points at a door that is not on the floor plan. Six named bouts happen on a taped circle in a warehouse — Reyes teaches, Dag answers a miss with a marked heavy, Pike repeats your last three punches — with a hub between fights, a loading-dock beat, and EN/IT card copy drawn from a single `STR` table capped at 55 words per card ([#1](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/1), [daea07a](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/daea07a)).

It is one HTML file against a tree-shaken `@babylonjs/core` 9.30.0 build plus the glTF loader, no build step to play, and it runs from the files on disk: Google Fonts when online, system fonts when not. Keyboard and standard gamepad both work, touch gets a move stick and a button cluster, and dropping a `.glb` on the title card swaps the auditor for an imported avatar while every other fighter is built in code. Apache-2.0 with a `NOTICE`, sounds CC0.

The last two sprints were release work rather than features, and the numbers are the point of them. The back half of the card was not a curve at all — the bot won 95% of Voss fights and 75% of Chen's on 200 seeded fights at Normal — so two `BOUTS` rows were retuned to 36% and 30%, giving a monotonic 90.5 / 67.5 / 44 / 36 / 30 / 22% across the six foes ([b49ad0f](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/b49ad0f), [5ffa37a](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5ffa37a), [#3](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/3)). Alongside it came a headless-Chrome regression suite at 22/22 covering bouts, the hub and dock, input and an offline `file://` run, axe and Lighthouse probes on local devDependencies, `lang`, charset and a main landmark on the page, a card stamp whose contrast survives the paper background, an offline zip target, and fullscreen on the first intro for touch and narrow viewports with a `sublevel-fs` opt-out and no throw on iOS ([#2](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/2), [5aa08fe](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5aa08fe)).
