---
title: "alien-hunt: an offline tune took the share of draws that reach the fixed function from 22 to 29 percent"
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [tool, security]
---

[alien-hunt](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt) tuned its candidate pool offline: no model was called and no embedding computed, and nothing from a target repository ran on the host — `scripts/pool_ceiling.py` only parses target code with `ast`, rebuilds atoms and pairs with alien-hunt's own `extract_atoms`, `colocated_pairs` and `pool_pairs`, and records whether a pair touches a function the fix changed. The cases come from GitHub's advisory database: reviewed, not withdrawn, PyPI, with a fix commit in the package's own repository, split at advisories published before 2025-01-01 into 159 tuning and 94 held-out snapshots ([reports/offline-tune/SUMMARY.md](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt/blob/master/reports/offline-tune/SUMMARY.md)).

At the pipeline's 20-pair limit, the share of draws containing a pair that reaches a fixed function went **22.2% → 28.8% held out** (92 cases) and **24.7% → 34.3% on the tuning split** (138); the share of kept pairs touching the fix rose from 3.7% to 4.6%, so 95 of every 100 offered pairs still miss it. Four changes were kept — record the function each atom sits in, draw pairs that reach new functions first, break ties toward busier functions, keep the atoms of the heaviest functions instead of every Nth — and two more were rejected for raising the tuning number while lowering the held-out one ([9e04348](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt/commit/9e04348), [60ee797](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt/commit/60ee797)).

The ceiling turns out to be extraction, not selection: with the 80/80/40 caps removed an atom sits in a fixed function in 95.7% of held-out cases, with the caps and the new retention 57.6%, and about half the remaining misses are fixes in code that yields no atom at all. The confirmation check is also much harder to fool after four red-team reads — scripts passing the syntax stage fell 43 → 9, 18 → 13, 18 → 12 and 17 → 2 — although the shapes that still pass are listed rather than closed, and one measurement bug (`--src` silently measuring the installed copy) was found and fixed on the way ([d89ec06](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt/commit/d89ec06), [7d89712](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt/commit/7d89712)).
