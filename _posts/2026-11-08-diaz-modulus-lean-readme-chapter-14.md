Read alone, the first sentence of [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean)'s README suggested the repository had no mathematical results of its own — a formal mirror of someone else's proof and nothing more, which was never quite true and is plainly false since the [prior-art sweep of chapter 14](/blog/2026/11/08/diaz-modulus-lean-prior-art-sweep/). It now says the library does have results of its own and links that chapter of the blueprint, the list of results not found in the sources read with the non-routine ones ranked, and keeps the caveat that a result nobody found is not thereby new; the later mention of the chapter gets the same link ([28ac275](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/28ac275), [#4](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/pull/4)).
