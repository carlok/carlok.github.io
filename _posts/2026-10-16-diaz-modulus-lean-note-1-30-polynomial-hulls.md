The Diaz companion note is at [v1.30](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.30), which states the R16 batch as a single result: Proposition 5.21, ten new nodes and milestones 137–139, after which [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) holds all 428 results on Lean's three standard axioms and the blueprint 211 nodes, with 195 appendix identifiers checked against the board ([2136117](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/2136117)). Clause (a) is Theorem C: for `u ∉ Q̄` with `uū` algebraic and `P ∈ Q̄[X]`, the hull `H₀ + Q̄P(u) + Q̄P(ū)` carries a 2×3 configuration exactly when `deg P` is 2 or 3 — the configurations themselves are Diaz's (2007, Théorème 7(1); 2004, Théorème 2), and the new half is the converse, which normalises a configuration to Laurent polynomials so that the orders at `0` and the degrees pinch every product's support into `[-1,1]`, the order sets of the hull being `{−k, −1, 0, 1, m}` and a sizes-2-and-3 sumset inside one of those being rigid. Clause (b) is that real polynomial expressions in `Re u` and `Im u` with algebraic coefficients carry no configuration in their hull, so Roy's theorem cannot exclude `(Re u)²` one number at a time; (c) and (d) are the inversion lemma — over `K ⊆ F`, a configuration in `V₀ + Kw + Kw⁻¹` with `V₀ ⊆ F` and `w` transcendental over `F` has its products in `V₀` — and its barrier, that `ℒ̃ + Q̄u + Q̄ū` carries no configuration when `u` is transcendental over the field of `ℒ̃`, so Roy's theorem alone cannot refute such a point. All ten results are routine by the blueprint's own rubric, and none of them is a step towards Diaz's conjecture: they say what Roy's theorem cannot do.

The same day's [research note v2](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/7f72e9d) — TeX only, after an adversarial multi-agent review — goes further and adds **Theorem E**, a classification of every polynomial in `u` and `ū` into three classes: (P), which is Theorem C; (Q), the family in the exponents `±2, ±3` such as `Re(u³) + cu²`, whose configurations have a factor whose involution exchanges `0` and `∞`; and (R), the project's real pencil spaces, the only class left once an exponent reaches 4 in absolute value. It is not formalised, no person has refereed it, and its literature check reached abstracts and quotations only, which the note says rather than hides.

Two attribution corrections came with the batch, both carried by note v1.29 (never released separately) and applied to four Prove2Me pages and read back: the configurations of the R14 and R15 spaces are Diaz's Théorème 7(1) at `v = u`, so only their invisibility to Theorem B, and the cyclic space's lack of a Möbius ratio, were not in the sources read — which walks back part of [yesterday's v1.27 and v1.28 account](/blog/2026/10/15/diaz-modulus-lean-notes-1-27-1-28-r15/) rather than adding to it — and the research notes' three degree-two families are defined for every involution of the u-line commuting with conjugation, where those texts said `u ↦ −u`, the involution of the formal members only. The [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) board carries both entries from the research side, the R16 mission with its ten theorem uuids and the correction that reaches four spaces ([318911d](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/318911d), [f79f13f](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/f79f13f)).
