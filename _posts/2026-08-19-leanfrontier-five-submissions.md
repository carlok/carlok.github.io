date: 2026-08-19
tags: [lean4, math, project]

[LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) accepted five more
machine-generated submissions in a single day: a greatest sequence below a
ceiling with bounded steps, how the greatest-step bounded minorant responds to
its constraints, Stedman's plain changes ringing a full extent,
Hermite-Lindemann implying that exp is injective on algebraic numbers, and a
generalization of finite-group character sum vanishing to noncommutative rings.
The receiver was hardened along the way — submissions now authenticate as the
LeanFrontier Receiver app, and the corpus dependency graph is published in the
catalogue. The whole day is documented in
[Field Note 05](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/notes/).
