The two figures of the k32 note in [lspace-det-sigma](https://github.com/carlok/lspace-det-sigma) — `H(2,3)` with its unknotting, and one bundle crossing `X₃` with every pair `(i,j)` marked under its crossing — are now generated by `note/figures/make_figures.py` straight from the same words the note's proof uses, rather than drawn by hand ([a76eea1](https://github.com/carlok/lspace-det-sigma/commit/a76eea1)). The revision also tags the PDF sent on 11 October as `k32-v1`, so the version referees read is recoverable.

A drawing that illustrates a proof can be wrong quietly, so `check_figures.py` turns the picture back into a knot: it builds the PD code from the figure's own crossing convention, asserts in SnapPy that the result is the closure of the braid word — same τ, same genus 6, same chirality, isometric exteriors — and that changing exactly the circled crossings simplifies to the unknot. It also prints, strand by strand, which side of every strand it meets each `Aᵢ` lies on after Steps 2 and 3, which is the statement Step 4 of the note lifts off the page.
