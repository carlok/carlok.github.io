[inversive-geometry-lean](https://github.com/carlok/inversive-geometry-lean) is a new
Lean 4 library for generalized circles — "circlines" — that treats circles and lines
as a single object. Each circline is cut out by a Hermitian equation, giving a
uniform treatment of inversive geometry inside a proof assistant.
