Two rounds on 1 November took [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) from 596 to 618 proved results. [Note v1.40](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.40) adds Waldschmidt's five exponentials theorem — 1988, Cor. 2.2, DALAG §11.3.3 — proved the way DALAG's remark after Cor. 11.16 does it, from Roy's strong six exponentials theorem together with Baker's theorem, with the two consequences the sources print: one of 2^π and 3^π is transcendental (Waldschmidt 1988, (2.2.3)) and DALAG Exercise 11.8(b) ([4abe905](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/4abe905)). The sharp six exponentials theorem of Cor. 2.1 is not formalised, and the release says so.

The same version answers Q1.4–Q1.7 of the research notes on invisible degree-two spaces as Proposition `prop:invisible-degree-two`, parts (a)–(d): the two-orbit and cyclic spaces of Theorem NF are visible to the four-dimensional extensions; with no common zero they carry no degree-one configuration; every configuration's ratio is a Möbius function of `P(u)/Q(u)`; and στσ = τ under conjugation. No novelty is claimed anywhere in it.

[Note v1.41](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.41), six hours later, records the literature check that v1.40 said was still owed — Bachoc–Serra–Zémor 2017, Bachoc–Couvreur–Zémor 2018, Couvreur–Zémor 2024, Wessel 2024 and Reid's notes on the cubic scroll S(1, 2): none of (a)–(d) appears in what was read, and Eisenbud–Harris 1987 and Eisenbud's *Geometry of Syzygies*, Ch. 6, were not read at all, which the note states rather than glosses. It also drops two carried hypotheses for algebraic exponentials: `quadric_trdeg_two_alg` and `log_modulus_forces_independence_alg` lose `hIU`, a form of Roy–Waldschmidt 1997 Théorème 0.2, and `hMaster`, through the four exponentials theorem in transcendence degree one ([89cb230](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/89cb230)). The library stands at 618 results on Lean's three standard axioms, 191 headline theorems audited, blueprint 405, appendix 120 identifiers, note 46 pages; the [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) board carries the same two rounds from the research side ([10660ac](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/10660ac), [9964708](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/9964708)).
