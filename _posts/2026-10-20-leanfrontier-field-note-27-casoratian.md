[Field Note 27](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/notes/field-note-27.html), *Three Days to a Consumer.*, records a day whose three submissions all build on the corpus by both of the series' reuse measures. [Banks' theorem and the four standalone subjects](/blog/2026/10/17/leanfrontier-field-note-26-roadmap/) were the previous week's shape; here three submissions from [qazW12345](https://github.com/qazW12345) take up definitions that were already accepted. [#532](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/532) proves the characteristic-polynomial shift law: if `f(n+2) = s·f(n+1) + t·f(n)` then its weighted binomial transform satisfies `g(n+2) = (s + 2a)·g(n+1) + (t − sa − a²)·g(n)`, which is `X² − sX − t` translated to `P(X − a)`, in any commutative ring and with no division — and it consumes `binomialTransform` from [#512](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/512), accepted three days earlier.

The other two return to Horadam sequences, which the corpus has carried since September. [#533](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/533) gives the division-free Binet formula in terms of the characteristic roots: for arbitrary `r, s, c, d` the sequence with `P = r + s`, `Q = r·s` and starting values `c + d`, `c·r + d·s` is exactly `c·rⁿ + d·sⁿ`, with the difference-of-powers identity `rⁿ − sⁿ` as the companion and no need for distinct roots, invertibility or a field. [#534](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/534) proves the discrete Abel–Liouville identity: for two solutions of the same variable-coefficient recurrence, the Casoratian `D n = u n·v(n+1) − u(n+1)·v n` satisfies `D(n+1) = q n·D n` and hence `D n = (∏ k < n, q k)·D 0`, which applied to two Horadam sequences gives `Qⁿ` times the determinant of their starting values. All three were machine-authored by GPT-6 on Mathlib v4.35.0, accepted by the trusted receiver with empty diagnostics, and the corpus stands at 120 modules and 96 internal import edges.

The probes read the new work honestly: three statements were really probed as written and no bounded tactic closed any of them, while [#534](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/534)'s three are reported as not elaborated from the baseline rather than as failures, because they use the module's own new `casoratian` definition ([#541](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/541), [7396267](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/commit/7396267)).
