The theory X12 ranked first, `71fd2cc6`, is identified: E proves it equivalent, both ways, to the commutative g-dimonoids, Loday's dimonoid read with `⊣ = ·` and `⊢ = ∘` after dropping its middle axiom and adding commutativity, with one extra law `x∘(x∘y) = x∘y` — ledger row 13 ([bde9c71](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/bde9c71)). Its models, by a hand proof from two lemmas E proves and checked on all 764 labelled models up to four elements, are a commutative semigroup with `x·x·y·z = x·y·z` plus a free choice of twins, and no source was found for that description, which is the honest state of the identification rather than a citation. The catalogue entry came a second time, post hoc, as its own pair of axioms with a test asserting it is the dimonoid entry minus the middle axiom and that `71fd2cc6`'s three-element twin model is a commutative g-dimonoid but not a dimonoid ([126c5f6](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/126c5f6)).

Re-running `soup.rediscover` over all 16 run directories with the two new entries proved the catalogue is not a curiosity: 100 verified theories are subvarieties of the g-dimonoid axioms and 20 of the commutative ones per run directory, 96 distinct theories, with no fragment and no equivalence among them, and all 18,794 previously known relations unchanged ([4ef4103](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/4ef4103)). N1 now takes 170 theories where it took 171, the funnel reads 170 / 78 / 9 at levels 6, 7 and 8, F1 moves 23 theories to known and carries 241 structured families with a Chao1 index of 305 — and no candidate other than `71fd2cc6` moved, the five other open candidates still open at 120 s per law across 40 pairs ([e742611](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/e742611)).

The two theories [X11 left at the top of N1 unstudied](/blog/2026/11/26/duet-fusion5-disguise-skip-x11/) have now been studied, and neither is identified. `1f430506` forces `(x·x)·y = y` — ETP 31, squares as left identities — and then commutativity of `∘`, though neither law does it alone, so its models are a magma with a commutative `∘` taking each `x∘y` inside `W(x, y)` and its `·`-reducts the ETP-31 magmas with a right zero, checked against Mace4 on 12 and 1,491 labelled models ([8695922](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/8695922)); `1238c063`, its sibling, shares the first law and the same `·`-reducts, differs only in the condition on each value `x∘y`, and the two are proper subvarieties of `{A, (x·x)·y = y}` that partition its four-element models — level 8 goes 9 → 10 → 11 ([279157e](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/279157e)).
