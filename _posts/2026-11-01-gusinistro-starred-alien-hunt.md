[gusinistro](https://github.com/gusinistro) put a star on [alien-hunt](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt) on 1 November — its first, and the twenty-seventh star across these repositories. The profile carries no name and no bio, and only two repositories, both of them working experiments rather than portfolios: [cctv-dvr-spacetimedb](https://github.com/gusinistro/cctv-dvr-spacetimedb), a DVR and CCTV monitoring system in TypeScript on SpacetimeDB with some AI features, and [voxel4d](https://github.com/gusinistro/voxel4d), a C++17 proof of concept for sparse voxel reconstruction, DDA traversal and Doppler-field sampling.

That reads as the same instinct alien-hunt is built on: something counts only when it can be observed directly — a reproducer that runs, a traversal that is traced through the volume — rather than inferred from a document.
