title: "carlok/sunslide was forked by sigco3111, for a Korean translation"
date: 2026-11-17
tags: [github, games]
---

[carlok/sunslide](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide) was forked by [sigco3111](https://github.com/sigco3111) on 17 November as [sunslide-ko](https://github.com/sigco3111/sunslide-ko) — "Sunslide 한국어판 (Korean translation of carlok/sunslide, Apache-2.0)". The login is not a passer-by: it runs a standing programme of Korean versions of browser games, 436 public repositories including `cadle-ko`, `gitvana-ko`, `plinkfx` and `harbor-gp-ko`, each a localisation of somebody else's game, alongside its own three.js work in `bounce-knight`, `windhill-rpg` and `protein-universe`.

[The game shipped Apache-2.0](/blog/2026/11/15/sunslide-public/) two days earlier, which is what lets a fork carry a translation legally without anyone asking. Whether it goes anywhere shows up in the fork's commits rather than here.
