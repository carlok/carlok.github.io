Contributed to [hrodrig/gghstats](https://github.com/hrodrig/gghstats) with a
[pull request](https://github.com/hrodrig/gghstats/pull/16) that adds selectable
daily clone statistics to the repository index: a second "Unique" line on the
clones-over-time chart with its legend enabled, plus a compact daily clone panel.
The change makes unique cloners visible alongside total clone events.
