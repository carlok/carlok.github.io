[Taermoon](https://github.com/Taermoon) starred [LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) on 23 November, its ninth star, the same day three pull requests from his fork were merged into it ([#558](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/558), [#559](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/559), [#560](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/560)).

The account is old and quiet: created in January 2016, three public repositories and no profile text, and one of them is [pequalnp-proofs](https://github.com/Taermoon/pequalnp-proofs), Lean 4 proofs from the public AI attempt at P versus NP, with open gaps inviting pull requests. Someone working on machine-checked proofs of an open problem, who arrived at the corpus with three ordinary combinatorics results and [a fork](/blog/2026/11/23/leanfrontier-forked-taermoon/), is the reader this corpus was built for.
