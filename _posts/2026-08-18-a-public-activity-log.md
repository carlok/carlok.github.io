This blog is a running, public log of activity across my GitHub repositories.

Each entry summarizes a release, a meaningful commit, or a new project — the
kind of change worth a sentence in public. Routine chores, typo fixes, and
automated merge noise are deliberately left out, and nothing private ever
appears here.

The log is updated by a daily check over my public repositories, and this site
is rebuilt automatically on every update.
