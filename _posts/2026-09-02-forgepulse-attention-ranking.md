[forgepulse](https://github.com/carlok/forgepulse) merged its first pull
request, [PR #1](https://github.com/carlok/forgepulse/pull/1): human attention
is now the default explainable ranking alongside clone volume. The dashboard
persists fork history, collects release/tag and Actions-checkout signals, and
shows each traffic diagnosis with its evidence inline. Clone-volume mode also
gained sortable Stars/Views/Clones column headers
([c69739b](https://github.com/carlok/forgepulse/commit/c69739b)).
