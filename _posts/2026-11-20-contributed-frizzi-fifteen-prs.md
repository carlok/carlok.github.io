[Frizzi](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi) is zazzati's passwordless tool for finding a date that works: the organiser creates a poll and gets a private management link by email, guests see the group's availability and vote, and everyone gets a link to edit their own answer — SvelteKit and TypeScript in front, Rust and Axum with PostgreSQL behind. On 20 November [carlok](https://github.com/carlok) opened fifteen pull requests against it and all fifteen were merged the same evening, +21,149 lines and −4,597: CI with pinned toolchains ([#1](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/1)), a portable e2e harness that no longer depends on how Mailpit gets installed ([#2](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/2)), Rust unit tests for templates, calendar and deadlines ([#3](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/3)) and Vitest coverage for the frontend logic ([#4](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/4)).

Then the correctness work: rate limiting per client address and per event with configurable SMTP TLS ([#6](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/6)), PDF rendering moved outside the transaction and notifications batched ([#5](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/5)), a repair pass over email time formats, outbox failures, retention and ICS output ([#7](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/7)), frontend fixes that stop a remount on refresh, clear stale form state and give the site a 404 page ([#8](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/8)), ICU plurals with the browser's language on first visit and no flash of Italian ([#9](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/9)), and a WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility pass over contrast tokens, radios, focus management and the calendar ([#10](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/10)).

The refactors carry "no behaviour change" in their titles and were reviewed that way: `lib.rs` split into modules with typed status and mail kinds ([#11](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/11)), real SvelteKit routes and components in place of one catch-all page ([#14](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/14)), and the frontend moved to Svelte 5 runes ([#15](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/15)), alongside resized illustrations, a subset Inter and cached `Intl` formatters ([#13](https://github.com/zazzati/frizzi/pull/13)). The work went through a fork first, where two dry-run CI pull requests ran the whole pipeline before anything reached upstream ([carlok/frizzi#1](https://github.com/carlok/frizzi/pull/1)).
