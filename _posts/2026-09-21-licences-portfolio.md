---
title: "Licences: MIT for the small tools, CC BY 4.0 for the prose"
date: 2026-09-21
tags: [github, meta]
---

An MIT licence was added to repositories that had been public without one:
[cold-path-server-podman](https://github.com/carlok/cold-path-server-podman/commit/30a7dbc),
[llm-source-security-review](https://github.com/carlok/llm-source-security-review/commit/61f8bf8),
[spriter](https://github.com/carlok/spriter/commit/2881055),
[lifechess](https://github.com/carlok/lifechess/commit/f9fc036),
[eclipse-3d-sim](https://github.com/carlok/eclipse-3d-sim/commit/84ad4e6) — whose
README was rewritten in the same commit — plus
[book](https://github.com/carlok/book/commit/8544a5e) and
[daily-drift-run](https://github.com/carlok/daily-drift-run/commit/5fd1453).
[solids-hunter](https://github.com/carlok/solids-hunter/commit/2f6f7a3) had a
licence file whose text had drifted from canonical MIT; the canonical text is
restored and the third-party carve-out it had absorbed lives in `THIRD-PARTY.md`,
where it belonged.

Two of those repositories hold writing as well as code, and they split the grant
accordingly: [book](https://github.com/carlok/book/commit/14f5242) and
[daily-drift-run](https://github.com/carlok/daily-drift-run/commit/3d04fe1) are
now [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) for the prose, with
MIT still covering the code that builds it.
