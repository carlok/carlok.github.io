[dratify](https://github.com/carlok/dratify) tagged
[v0.1.4](https://github.com/carlok/dratify/tree/v0.1.4) and
[v0.1.5](https://github.com/carlok/dratify/tree/v0.1.5) after an audit of its
published numbers: the README's speed table came from CNF files that are not in
the repository, and its "~18x" was the top of a range quoted as the typical
value. A committed benchmark script
([bench/repro.py](https://github.com/carlok/dratify/blob/main/bench/repro.py))
now produces figures anyone can reproduce — a second run held the geometric
mean at ~15x while individual ratios moved between 11x and 22x, so the release
quotes the mean and states the spread as a spread. The same pass
([c34c31b](https://github.com/carlok/dratify/commit/c34c31b)) added a fuzz
workflow and stopped error messages from reporting version skew in the
vocabulary of an unrelated project's module.
