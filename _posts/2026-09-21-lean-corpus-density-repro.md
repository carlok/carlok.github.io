---
title: "lean-corpus-density pins the corpora its committed data was built from"
date: 2026-09-21
tags: [lean4, research]
---

The committed density data in
[lean-corpus-density](https://github.com/carlok/lean-corpus-density) now names the
corpora and revisions it was built from
([550a97d](https://github.com/carlok/lean-corpus-density/commit/550a97d)), so a
figure in the report can be traced back to a specific corpus state instead of to
the tool that read it, and the reproduction check of 21 September is recorded
alongside it
([31558d4](https://github.com/carlok/lean-corpus-density/commit/31558d4)). The
repository's claim is that machine mathematics accumulates; the data has to be
pinned before that claim is checkable by anyone else.
