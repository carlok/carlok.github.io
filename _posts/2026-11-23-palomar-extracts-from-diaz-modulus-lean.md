---
title: "Five more Palomar extracts from diaz-modulus-lean: four, five and strong six exponentials, Baker's theorem, Gel'fond's criterion"
date: 2026-11-23
tags: [lean4, math]
---

Five new repositories carry one theorem each out of [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean), prepared for the [Palomar registry](https://palomar-registry.org/): [five-exponentials-palomar](https://github.com/carlok/five-exponentials-palomar) (Waldschmidt 1988, Corollary 2.2), [four-exponentials-palomar](https://github.com/carlok/four-exponentials-palomar) (the four exponentials theorem in transcendence degree one, with Schneider's eighth problem — one of e^e and e^{e²} is transcendental), [strong-six-exponentials-palomar](https://github.com/carlok/strong-six-exponentials-palomar) (Roy's strong six exponentials theorem and his rank bound for matrices of logarithms), [baker-linear-forms-palomar](https://github.com/carlok/baker-linear-forms-palomar) (Baker's theorem and the Schneider–Lang criterion for ℂ^{d₀} × (ℂ^×)^{d₁} with d₀ ≤ 1) and [transcendence-criterion-palomar](https://github.com/carlok/transcendence-criterion-palomar) (Gel'fond's criterion in Waldschmidt's 1971 form). Each is a module-system extract of the library at `59d5a598c292`, changed only for `module`, `public import` and `public section`, with `Challenge.lean` stating the theorems under deliberate `sorry` and importing Mathlib alone, `Solution.lean` proving them, and `comparator.json` naming what the Comparator checks — 8 carried library modules for the transcendence criterion, 35 for Baker, 69 for four exponentials, 120 for strong six, 136 for five — on Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib v4.35.0, Apache-2.0.

What the five share is the metadata: every `formalization.yaml` is v0.4 and its `substantive_formalization` points at diaz-modulus-lean at that revision, so the registry is told that the extracts are the submission and the library is the substantive work. The Gel'fond statement carries the constants the formal proof actually reaches, C = max(10 + ε, (4 + ε) a₁ a₂), which its source note says may differ from the printed ones, and the four-exponentials note records that one hypothesis of Waldschmidt's Lemme 2, met in print with Gel'fond's two-logarithm bound, is made unnecessary by the separation-free proof.

The library moved the same way on 22 November: its README now says Palomar entries come from module-system extracts and marks its own non-module surface as reference only ([9c691e3](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/9c691e3)), citing the six exponentials registration that [the first extract received](/blog/2026/11/22/six-exponentials-lean-palomar-registration/) ([ea1a26b](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/ea1a26b)), and its Comparator job now runs the toolchain's bundled `lake comparator` — lean4export, leanchecker, NanoDa and con-ron have shipped with Lean since v4.35.0-rc2 — unmodified from Palomar's template, so a toolchain bump needs no separate pin ([59d5a59](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/59d5a59)).
