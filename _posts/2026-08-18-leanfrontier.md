date: 2026-08-18

[LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) is a new Lean 4 library of
machine-generated, kernel-verified mathematics. Every theorem it contains has been
checked by Lean's proof kernel rather than taken on faith, which means the
machine-generated results carry the same formal guarantees as hand-written proofs.
