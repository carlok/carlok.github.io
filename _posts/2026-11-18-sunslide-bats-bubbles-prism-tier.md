---
title: "sunslide: if you can see an awake bat it can catch you, hot-pink bubbles with a rare prism tier, and no shader compiles mid-play"
date: 2026-11-18
tags: [games, project]
---

A playtest pass on [sunslide](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide) fixed what players reported without changing the rules they were misreading. "Bats don't always sleep in sunshine" was true by design — only a bat whose island is *home* sleeps, daylight merely slows them — but daylit bats were drawn with droopy eyelids, so they looked asleep and bit anyway; waiting bats are now hidden, their roost shows a dashed ring in the bat's colour that pulses faster as it gets ready with a hollow dot on the minimap, and a puff and a sound play when a bat goes live. Contact is measured on the sphere by great-circle angle rather than the old flat-grid radius, which caught from a gap at the equator and let bodies overlap without a catch near the poles, and a shield ring after a respawn, sector start or sinkhole holds bats off — a live bat that touches a shielded Pip bounces back ([#51](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/pull/51), [dbb08c0](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/commit/dbb08c0)).

The yellow bubble seed sat ΔE 5–12 from the home walls, the slide glow and islands 6–8 — worse in colour-blind mode — so the seed is now hot pink `#ff1f8f` with a dark-inked shell rim by day and no yellow floor specks, and [`tests/unit/bubble-contrast.test.js`](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/blob/main/tests/unit/bubble-contrast.test.js) holds it at ΔE ≥ 40 from every island colour in both palettes, the glow, home walls and lamps, and ≥ 25 from the berry. Cell value 3 became a **prism bubble**: rainbow-faceted, worth five plain bubbles plus combo and doubled at home, two of them in sector 1 up to six, on islands far from the start, with their own sound, toast and minimap dot — and scoring moved into `src/logic/scoring.js` so the tiers are one thing to test.

The flashes and hitches had several one-frame causes. `ResizeObserver` resized after the frame was drawn, so a cleared canvas could be painted, and resizes now happen in the next animation frame; `pow` bases in the sky, cloud, aurora and pillar shaders got `max(0.0, …)` guards, because a single NaN pixel becomes a black square through bloom. Shader compilation left play entirely: every object is compiled at boot including the hidden ones, the bloom pipeline loads once instead of being re-created every frame until ready, its render-target variants are compiled before a swap that waits for a non-play moment, and tile materials are kept across sectors instead of being disposed and recompiled at each one — on SwiftShader, `renderer.info.programs` went from 73 → 69 → 71 → 71 → 71 → 72 churning through title, play, first bloom, sinkhole and next sector to **77 flat**, with the auto-quality fix judging quality against the display's cadence ([2fabd04](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/commit/2fabd04), [7c3ad78](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/commit/7c3ad78), [f01da80](https://github.com/carlok/sunslide/commit/f01da80)).
