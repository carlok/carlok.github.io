Contributed to
[leanprover-community/mathlib4](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4):
[PR #43660](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43660) was
merged into master on Sep 10, weakening unused hypotheses in
`Mathlib/Data/Finset/Pairwise.lean`, `Mathlib/Order/Antichain.lean` and
`Mathlib/Order/Monotone/Extension.lean` — five one-line binder changes, five
insertions and five deletions. It answers
[a request on the mathlib Zulip](https://leanprover.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/287929-mathlib4/topic/unused.20hypotheses.3A.20431.20in.20a.2029k.20sample.2C.20and.2033.20one-line.20PRs/near/623003643)
to slice the machine-found weakenings into smaller, reviewable PRs, and the
[unused-assumptions](https://github.com/carlok/unused-assumptions) pipeline
supplied the edits: replace one binder with a weaker class, keep the proof
unchanged, let the compiler decide. The larger
[#43503](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/pull/43503) — 32
typeclass weakenings across 29 files — is still open.
