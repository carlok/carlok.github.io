[staffarda](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda) is public: a procedural Blender model of the ground floor of the *foresteria* — the guesthouse — of Staffarda Abbey in Revello, Piedmont, the two-nave hall with four stone columns and a brick rib vault that once served as the pilgrims' refectory. It was rebuilt from three phone photos taken on 16 October, with no survey, point cloud or photo projection: every wall, rib, capital and brick is generated by a Python script, and `blender/build_foresteria.py` keeps every dimension, colour and option as a constant at the top ([883d0bc](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/883d0bc)). The README is explicit about what that is worth — dimensions come from the perspective in the photos and are good to about 10–15%, and the result is a plausible, stylized reconstruction rather than a measured one.

The hall is two naves by five square bays, roughly 10.8 × 27 m, springing at 2.5 m with keystones about 6.4 m up; the ribs spring higher on the side walls than on the columns, from small corbels, as measured on the photos. Each material carries its own procedural weathering — flaking plaster, stained vault cells, mottled gneiss — and the herringbone floor is about 7,800 individual worn cotto bricks ([2cc47a3](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/2cc47a3)). What the photos could not settle is a build switch rather than a guess: the windows are modelled with the flat lintels and round-headed lights they have today, and the arched-head hypothesis is available as `WINDOW_HEADS=arched` ([97bb8d5](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/97bb8d5)).

`blender/structural_check.py` is plain Python with no Blender in it — a first-order thrust-line check of the vault and its supports that reads its dimensions from the build script — and `blender/walkthrough.py` renders the hall as one slow continuous take under two minutes, in through the doorway and slaloming between the columns at eye height, with stops to look around ([9679dd3](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/9679dd3)). The last round added a compare mode that renders eight key moments as both EEVEE and Cycles stills and fixed a springer flicker by widening the wall springer blocks 2 cm past the transverse arches, whose coplanar side faces had been z-fighting ([697dbaa](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/697dbaa), [cad4aaf](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/cad4aaf)).
