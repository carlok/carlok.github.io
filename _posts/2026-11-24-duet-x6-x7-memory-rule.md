---
title: "duet: X6 and X7, disguises down to 2.2%, and a memory leak that was the verifiers and not the corpus copies"
date: 2026-11-24
tags: [math, research]
---

Run X6 of [duet](https://github.com/carlok/duet) was stopped by its own memory rule at 12:26 UTC at 72 % of its budget, with 1,460 theories verified, 645 scored and 4.0 million evaluations: none of the 645 is a fragment, and the parameter-definition screen added for this run — a definition with a constant square — fired 356 times, having caught 18 of the 20 candidates X5 produced and none of the other 138 ([4046df2](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/4046df2)). Three of its four preregistered predictions held, and the fourth failed in the good direction, with 9 of the top twenty classical; the twenty candidates fall into 17 varieties, and the four at N1 ranks 1–4 were studied closely and none identified — `3818b88f` is equivalent, both ways, to a totally symmetric quasigroup with an idempotent selector `x∘(x·y) = x∘y`, and its models come from TS-quasigroups of order at most four ([f491a61](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/f491a61), [3029422](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/3029422)).

X7 added a fourth arm — the X5 filters and the X6 screen, a skip over the known catalogue and ledger, a skip over 38,701 keys seen before, and a distinct level-0 bitmap — and the supervisor's memory rule stopped it at 2.65 hours: 737 verified, 511 scored, no fragments, 2.2 % disguises against X6's 24 %, 12 of 20 candidates disconnected, 19 varieties, Q1, Q3 and Q4 holding and Q2 failing at 73.8 % distinct ([6548de9](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/6548de9)). The leak behind both stops was in the verifiers rather than in the searchers' corpus copies: 250 MB growing to 1.4 GB private per process, so a verifier now recycles itself above `--recycle-mb` by exiting 75 and the supervisor restarts it, with the smoke test and all 80 tests passing ([b2eefce](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/b2eefce)).

The funnel over every run stands at 59.5 million cheap evaluations, 7,476 verified, 161 varieties and 36 candidates at level 7, and the writing split with it: `intro.pdf` is now the text alone — nine pages of definitions, examples, theorems and proofs — and `exercises.pdf` the exercises by section with their solutions, both built from one shared preamble ([9ce7719](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/9ce7719)).
