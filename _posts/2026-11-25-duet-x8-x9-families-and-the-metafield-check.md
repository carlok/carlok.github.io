---
title: "duet: X8 passes 100M on volume and fails its distinctness prediction, X9 lets verifiers do the searching, and the project states what it is not"
date: 2026-11-25
tags: [math, research]
---

Run X8 of [duet](https://github.com/carlok/duet) was the search-heavy one: eight searchers kept alive by a new `--min-searchers`, three verifiers, `--recycle-mb 700`, a 1 GB memory stop and nine hours toward 100 million evaluations, with `soup.known` seeded from X4a and X7's 43,705 seen-before keys ([041912c](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/041912c)). It bought volume and little else. Level 0 passed 100M — 115.1M over the run and its x8b continuation, which spent the remaining 28,217 s — so R1 held, and R3's parameter screen stayed under 5 %, but R2 failed at 66.2 % distinct against the 70 % it predicted and R4 failed at 0.9 % for a largest new family against 2 %, with 444 theories verified, 192 scored and 1,256 left in the queue when the memory rule stopped it ([ade7f80](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/ade7f80)).

The after-run reading is where the run paid: 17 of the 20 candidates do not split, 9 are disconnected, and 218 candidates dedupe to 180 varieties whose top two scores are the same theory, while `7e9c798d` is a parameter disguise V6's screen did not catch. X8's best, `3eecc7a6`, sits at N1 rank 10 and the top nine are unchanged, so the funnel only moved from 36 to 43 at level 7. The F1 arm, which asks for *families* of verified theories rather than individual finds, gave the better answer: 124 structured families with a Chao1 index of 138, and structured theories at 0–0.5 % of early runs against 44–69 % of X5–X7 — direction mattered more than volume, since the prior arms spent 51M evaluations for 6 families and the fusion arms 12.5M for 118 ([a756dc5](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/a756dc5)). The laws emerging across families are `x(yx)=y`, `x(xx)=y(xy)` and commutativity, E confirms 96.8 % of corpus-implied properties, and `de0c0cf3` turned out to be a proper supervariety of `3818b88f` with ∘ reversed, witnessed on three elements.

Two new tools came with it. `soup.linear` searches models of the form `x·y = ax + by + c`, `x∘y = dx + ey + f` over Z/n and GF(q) up to 64 — every term is an affine form, so a law holds exactly when the coefficients agree — and it refuted 14 of 116 open claims on 8 to 17 elements in the post-run sweep ([3825939](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/3825939)). `soup.lean_cert` now emits packed Nat literals by default, the encoding [packed-table-footprint](/blog/2026/11/21/packed-table-footprint-no-axioms/) measured at zero axioms, with the 27 committed modules unchanged. The paper's claim was also cut back to what can be defended: a memo in `notes/metafield.md` records that open-ended search, automated mathematician systems, Wolfram's ruliology, Le Floch's 456 semigroup varieties, SemiBase and murmurations all came first, and that what was not found in print is the combination — search over two-operation law sets with synergy as the target, a taxonomy of the theories found, and conjectures across families ([4bc85dc](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/4bc85dc), [20492f4](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/20492f4)).

X9 then inverted the run: no searcher at the start, seven verifiers, and a fork of X8's corpus and queue, with V7 adding no z3 run after E has already proved a claim and the linear models for scoring ([924bb28](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/924bb28)). A stage-b benchmark on 24 of X8's theories showed 58 % less stage-b time, five claims changing to linear refutations and no score changing, and the four predictions all held: 1,255 of the 1,256 copied theories got a result at 88.8 per verifier-hour against x8b's 16.9, the linear screen refuted 60 of 354 stage-b claims, and stage c stayed under its 35-minute cap — though only as written, since two tasks were empty and the other five took 44 minutes ([588a867](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/588a867)). The memory rule stopped it at 2.0 h once every verifier had taken a stage-c task, which is also why no searcher ever started, and the post-run sweep found nothing further.
