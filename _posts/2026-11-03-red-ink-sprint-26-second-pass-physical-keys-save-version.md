Once all six bouts are won, [red-ink](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink)'s title card offers "Second pass: bout N": the same six fighters at the player's own difficulty, each with one change — Reyes iron jaw at 1.4× health, Dag quick hands with reaction 0.8× and cooldown 0.85×, Pike and Voss heavier hands at 1.1× and 1.25× damage, Chen relentless at 0.8× cooldown, Halvorsen iron jaw at 1.06× health — with the HUD reading "BOUT n / 6 · II", wins counting only in `progress.pass2`, and the save moving to version 2 with a migrate step that adds the field ([e2a32bf](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/e2a32bf)). Measured with the new `balance-run --pass2`, 200 bot meets on Normal and seed 1: the first pass is identical at 90.5 / 67.5 / 44.0 / 36.0 / 30.0 / 22.0 while the second reads 71.5 / 52.0 / 16.5 / 16.0 / 12.0 / 11.5, and the plan's Hard multipliers had to be dropped — Hard alone takes the bot to 0 against Pike and Halvorsen — as did block and dodge changes, which did not move the bot at all.

Movement now follows the physical key: `event.code` KeyW/KeyA/KeyS/KeyD moves the fighter whatever the caps are labelled, so AZERTY and Dvorak get WASD under the fingers, a key typed W from the Z position or a comma on Dvorak no longer moves, and the hints say ZQSD where Chromium's `getLayoutMap` reports other caps — jab, heavy, block and dodge stay by typed letter, and none of it was tried on a real AZERTY board ([15399bf](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/15399bf)). Four flow fixes came with it: a Continue action on the title card for a player with one to five wins ([0fa1e82](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/0fa1e82)), Enter standing aside when focus is on any other button, link or field ([38e2472](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/38e2472)), a win card that says the win will be lost when storage throws instead of dropping it in silence ([8da0c81](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/8da0c81)), and a pause-menu restart that starts at full health rather than healing to the damaged value plus 35 ([a831652](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/a831652)).

The save carries a version and a `migrateProgress` step now, so a sprint 25 save loads intact and is stamped on the next write, broken JSON gives the defaults, and a save from a newer build is kept exactly as it is ([d18fa99](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/d18fa99)); a pasted link gets a real preview, with a description, theme colour, SVG favicon and a 1200×630 `assets/social.jpg`, and the `twitter:card` tag was dropped the next day because the target is Instagram and it reads Open Graph ([c01fbba](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/c01fbba), [5f37b30](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5f37b30)). The repo also got an `AGENTS.md` of the rules a background agent must not break, and dependency care: grouped weekly Dependabot for npm and Actions with Babylon and esbuild deliberately ignored, a non-blocking `npm audit` workflow, and every action pinned to the commit SHA it resolves to ([cef5d59](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/cef5d59), [a618e39](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/a618e39)). One CI run failed on a test that read fighter state two frames after a key press, which is longer than the whole heavy windup on a slow runner, so it polls up to 40 frames instead; the sprint closes at 53 of 53 with the bout table and step probe unchanged ([fee32d1](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/fee32d1), [5d73833](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5d73833), [#20](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/20)).
