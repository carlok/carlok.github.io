---
title: "duet: X10 fails three of its five predictions, and half of what it verified was one shape in disguise"
date: 2026-11-26
tags: [math, research]
---

Run X10 of [duet](https://github.com/carlok/duet) was preregistered in blocks — 28,800 s in all, two stage-c tasks at a time with the search beside them — and came in at 28,812 s over two blocks ([fc768e7](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/fc768e7)). The first block was stopped by its own memory rule after 2,313 s, 642 MB available at the stop: two verifiers peaked together at 1,739 MB and 1,552 MB while another five-container stack of the user's, started beside the block, held about 1.1 GB that the workers' own PSS never counts — deviation 12, and the reason the blocks from x10b on ran with four searchers instead of six ([8e17bd4](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/8e17bd4), [c615956](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/c615956)). The second block then spent 26,530 s to budget with no memory stop and no stage-c task cut: 557 theories verified, 26.6 million surrogate evaluations ([aca4087](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/aca4087)).

Three of the five preregistered predictions failed, and two of the failures are about the queue rather than the search. U1 fell with x10a's memory stop. U2 asked for at least twelve of the 156 copied stage-c tasks to be reached and got two, because the stage-c lane is ordered by priority and X10's own new tasks outranked the copies. U3 asked for at least 5 % of stage-c claims to settle and got none of about 27, after the second block alone put 13.2 stage-c worker-hours in. U4 held at 28.3 million new evaluations against a 22 million bar its own amendments had already written off as unlikely, taking level 0 to 143.5 million, and U5 held at 578 verified theories ([notes/x10_predictions.out](https://github.com/carlok/duet/blob/main/notes/x10_predictions.out)).

What the run did find is a new attractor: 294 of those 578 theories, half of them, are parameter disguises of a single shape — ∘ a twist of a Steiner-type · by two constant squares — and the verifier's screen caught every one at about 8 s each, which is now the first item on the repository's open list, a search-side skip before another search run. Its 20 candidates merge into 14 new varieties, but only 4 are disconnected and 15 have models over 𝔽₄ or ℤ/6, and the best at N1, `ec36621c`, sits at rank 10, so the top nine stand as they were; a linear sweep over the run's 342 open claims refuted none ([fc768e7](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/fc768e7)). The funnel over every run reads 143.5 million cheap evaluations, 9,974 verified, 6,953 with a proved emergent law, 5,670 not a fragment of a named theory, 258 candidates in 214 varieties, 139 that no check recognises, 57 carrying every automatic mark and 9 studied closely and still unidentified — and structured theories were 11.0 % of X10, adding 3 new families to 154, with the plumbing left passing 103 tests ([ad6c5f2](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/ad6c5f2)).

The paper was rewritten the same night and cut by more than half: 7,064 words to 3,246 after a compile gave eight pages instead of seven, then to 2,824, organised by finding rather than by run — funnel table, calibration, the claims finite search cannot refute, rediscovery and disguises, attractors and the fusion arms, families, candidates, limitations — with every number brought up to the 25 November funnel ([b9d4827](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/b9d4827), [f0df8e8](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/f0df8e8)). The abstract now leads with the nine unidentified theories, each given its axioms or a plain E-equivalent reading and one line, before the funnel that produced them, the method, and the 26, 11 and 18 new families of X7 to X9; and the author is Carlo Perassi rather than the GitHub handle, with quadratula and parsimagma credited the same way ([b8bcf50](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/b8bcf50), [821f329](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/821f329)). The rebuilt PDF is seven pages with no undefined references ([02866e0](https://github.com/carlok/duet/commit/02866e0)).
