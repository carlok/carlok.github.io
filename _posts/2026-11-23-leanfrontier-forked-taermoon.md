[carlok/LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier) was forked by [Taermoon](https://github.com/Taermoon) at 07:25 UTC on 23 November, as [Taermoon/LeanFrontier](https://github.com/Taermoon/LeanFrontier) — thirteen minutes before the first pull request from it opened, the clique–coclique bound ([#558](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/558)).

The other two results followed the same morning and all three were merged between 19:52 and 20:13 UTC ([#559](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/559), [#560](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/560)), which makes Taermoon the first contributor since August other than qazW12345. [Field Note 29](/blog/2026/11/23/leanfrontier-field-note-29-new-contributor/) says what the three results are; the fork is the ordinary part of the story — a corpus that had one author for three months now has a second.
