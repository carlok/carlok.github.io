title: "dash: a serverless DMARC aggregate-report parser for Gmail"
date: 2026-08-22
tags: [email, security, tool, serverless]
---

[dash](https://github.com/carlok/dash) parses DMARC aggregate reports in a
serverless setup for Gmail: it extracts and parses incoming reports, enriches
the failing sources, and emails a summary of which senders are failing DMARC
and why. The repo is public and actively developed.
