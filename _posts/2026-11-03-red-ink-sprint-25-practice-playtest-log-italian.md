[red-ink](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink) gained a Practice link on the title card, shown once a bout has been won: it opens the opponent's dossier with Another opponent and Back, a practice fight starts at full health and grants no wins, unlocks or saved progress, and its card says so and offers Fight again or Choose opponent ([80d568d](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/80d568d)). Every finished bout now writes one entry to a playtest log — result, time, hits taken, blocks, dodges, punches thrown and landed, difficulty, who controlled it, block mode and the practice flag — keeping the newest 200 in browser storage, with Export playtest log and a two-tap Clear beside it in the title settings; the export is a JSON download, the clipboard if that fails, and nothing is sent anywhere ([a84e6df](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/a84e6df)).

An audit of every visible string with the language set to Italian turned up English in the HUD and menus: Sound on/off, BOUT n / 6, Got it, the touch buttons, the bind and difficulty buttons, the slider and checkbox labels, FIGHTER and the avatar texts, the key and pad strips, the Strike and Defend rows — which had also stopped following a rebind, because they were matched by their English label — the aria labels, and the BLOK comic word, now PARA! ([4cb3f45](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/4cb3f45)). All of it comes from `STR` now, the page sets `lang` on `<html>`, and the new test walks a deny list of English words over the page while requiring both languages for every entry; the HUD and touch controls fit at 390×844 and 844×390, and the reading of the title stays "Sublevel Three" in both languages.

The same sprint made block a toggle for players who cannot hold a key ([bfe6c3a](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/bfe6c3a)), offered Try Easy on the third straight loss on one bout, paused a fight with "Controller disconnected" when the pad in use is unplugged, put a card with a Reload button in place of a blank page when WebGL is missing or the GPU resets, and built the first-fight hint out of the actual binds or the pad and touch lines ([64365e9](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/64365e9)). The regression now runs in a workflow on every pull request and push to master, and the Pages deploy calls that workflow as a job the build needs, so a failing test stops a deploy ([5fe7cb1](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5fe7cb1), [b37fe9a](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/b37fe9a)). The draw count that had looked like a regression was noise: a worktree of the sprint 23 commit gave 39–42 draws against 41–44 now, and moved by two between its own runs, so the regression carries a budget instead of a fixed number ([bf82aa1](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/bf82aa1)); the sprint closes at 45 of 45 with the bout table and the step probe unchanged ([ac4d0f4](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/ac4d0f4), [#17](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/17)).
