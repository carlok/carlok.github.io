[jmikedupont2](https://github.com/jmikedupont2) — Mike DuPont, the full-stack developer behind the p2p/ML tooling at [meta-introspector](https://github.com/meta-introspector), whose [fork of aristowrap](/blog/2026/10/13/aristowrap-forked-meta-introspector/) and [star on it](/blog/2026/10/13/jmikedupont2-stars-aristowrap/) both landed on 13 October — starred [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) on 13 November, its first star.

A month between the two is the interesting part. The first visit was to the Lean-worker wrapper, which is plumbing an org can reuse; this one is to the companion note and the 628-result library it documents, which is not plumbing at all. The same account that put aristowrap on its list came back for the formalisation, the day [note v1.42](/blog/2026/11/14/diaz-modulus-lean-roy-corollary-one-no-hypothesis/) put Roy's Corollary 1 on no hypothesis.
