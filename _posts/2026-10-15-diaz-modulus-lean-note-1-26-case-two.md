The Diaz companion note is at [v1.26](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.26), which answers the two questions Proposition 5.11 left open: whether every conjugation-stable space invisible to Theorem 5.5 has a configuration factor of degree one, and which of the real pencil spaces `W(λ, f)` Theorem 5.5 can see. [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) ports the eleven new results (milestones 127–133) and the mirrored project now holds all 414 proved results on Lean's three standard axioms ([d749dbb](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/d749dbb), 46 files and about 4,700 lines).

**Proposition 5.12** builds the conjugation-stable space `Q̄ + Q̄u + Q̄ū + Q̄F + Q̄F̄` for `F = u³/s³ + u²/s²` with `s` real algebraic and `s² = uū`: spanned by the products of `(1, u²)` and `(ū, u + s, F̄)`, invisible to Theorem 5.5, and carrying no configuration with a Möbius ratio, so it is none of the spaces `W(λ, f)`. The two-orbit spaces `Q̄ + Q̄u + Q̄u⁻¹ + Q̄(α/(u − a) + β/(u + a)) + Q̄(γ/(u − b) + δ/(u + b))` carry a spanning configuration with factor `(1, u²)` and are equally invisible, and they are conjugation-stable exactly when `|a| = |b| = |u|` with `β/α` and `δ/γ` real. **Remark 5.13** closes the degree question from the other side: no configuration ratio has degree three or more, because if `1, u, u⁻¹` lie in `c·span(1, h, h², k, hk)` for `h = p(u)/q(u)` then `max(deg p, deg q) ≤ 2`, via the degree formula inside Mathlib's proof of Lüroth's theorem; for a real Laurent `f` of width `k` in `u/s`, the space `W(λ, f)` is invisible when `k ≥ 3` or when `k = 2` with `f(−λ̄) ≠ 0`, and contains `u²` and `ū²` when `k = 2` and `f(−λ̄) = 0`. The general criterion (visible iff complete) and the degree-two normal forms stay in the text, unformalised. The blueprint is at 197 nodes, and the same day's [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) entry carries the mission from the research side ([b6be820](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/b6be820)).
