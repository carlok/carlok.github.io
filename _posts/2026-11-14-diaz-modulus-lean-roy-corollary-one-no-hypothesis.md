[Note v1.42](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.42) of [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) formalises Roy's Corollary 1 in full generality and Roy's Theorem 4 with it — ten nodes, `Diaz.roy_rank_bound` and `Diaz.roy_theorem_four` plus eight steps, all on Lean's three standard axioms, with no hypothesis carried ([b5eb6ae](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/b5eb6ae)). The statement is the general d × l case the project had only in its 2 × 3 form: a matrix over ℒ̃ is equivalent over Q̄ to one whose top right block vanishes, with d·l′ ≤ rank(M)·(d′ + l′), which is Roy's lower bound rank(M) ≥ dθ/(1 + θ) ([note v1.42](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.42), 46 pages). The strong six exponentials theorem, proved without hypothesis on 30 October, is what this generalises.

The route is an induction on d along rational subspaces of maximal slope: when the subspace is semistable the linear subgroup theorem (DALAG Th. 11.21, already in the library from the multiplicity estimate) is applied once at Υ⁻¹(U), and otherwise ℂᵈ splits into two pieces with smaller d satisfying the same hypothesis. Two steps — the rank of the relations and the space of periods — were not found in the sources read in the form stated, and the note says both are routine rather than claiming them as new; DALAG and Roy both reach Theorem 4 through intermediate statements this route avoids. The library stands at 628 results with 201 headline theorems audited and the blueprint at 415.

The [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) board carries the research-side entry ([eaa50c9](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/eaa50c9)): the ten proofs come to 2,392 lines, six leaves were accepted outright and four sketches upgraded as their children closed, a paper proof was written first and checked by an independent referee, and a stub-free chain builds against the mirror's library on Mathlib v4.35.0. What is not proved is stated as plainly — only K = Q̄, not Roy's Theorem 5, none of the applications, and Diaz's conjecture itself remains open, with Cor. 1 the input a rank-four exclusion of u⁴ would need, which the memo of 2 November [showed rank three cannot supply](/blog/2026/11/02/diaz-modulus-lean-roy-rank-bound-rank-three/).
