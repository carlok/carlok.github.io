Three releases of the [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) companion note landed on 14 November, 628 results to 684. [Note v1.43](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.43) closes the gap the previous note left explicit — Roy's Theorem 5, DALAG Cor. 11.15 for Q̄ and ℒ̃, is now machine-checked with no hypothesis, with Waldschmidt's *Variations* Th. 3.1 and Cor. 2.14, *Further variations* Th. 2.1 and Th. 1.2, DALAG Cor. 12.21–12.24 and Fischler 2001 Th. 1.2 with Rémond's lemma on Lean's three standard axioms ([note v1.43](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.43), [b5eb6ae](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/b5eb6ae), [4f37b90](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/4f37b90)).

[v1.44](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.44) generalises *Further variations* Th. 2.1 from finite-dimensional Y to every Q̄-subspace Y, adds Roy's Lemma on page 40 in the closed form of φ — its floor being DALAG's Ψ — and Cor. 12.23 giving rank at least half the structural rank for entries in ℚ + ℒ ([2909b21](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/2909b21)). [v1.45](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.45) puts Cor. 11.15 in its printed form over (ℚ, ℚ + ℒ) with d_min, DALAG Lemma 12.9's structural rank through any affine map injective on the span of the entries, and Cor. 12.23 in its affine form, reaching 684 results, 257 audited headline theorems and a 471-result blueprint ([0acca6e](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/0acca6e)).

The part worth reading is [ERRATA.md](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/blob/master/ERRATA.md), 645 lines of printed statements and proofs met while formalising: *Variations* Cor. 2.13 is false as printed, with a counterexample in 2×3 matrices of logarithms (Λ₁₁ = 1, Λ₁₂ = Λ₁₃ = 0, Λ₂₁ = log 2, Λ₂₂ = Log(2 + i), Λ₂₃ = Log(3 + 2i)) satisfying the hypotheses while both differences are logarithms; the second clause of DALAG Lemma 11.18 (2) is false; the proof of Prop. 11.19 reads Th. 11.5 (3′) at the trivial subgroup, which (3′) need not select; Prop. 12.13's (iv) ⇒ (iii) uses a matrix whose entries need not lie in E; and Lemma 11.24's κ′ = 0 fails in 𝔾ₘ³. The errata also records the author's own web erratum for Cor. 2.12 and states which passages hold for Q̄ only — and the notes keep saying no novelty is claimed for any of it, since these are classical results proved as printed ([0d932ea](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/0d932ea)). The [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) board carries the research side of the three rounds ([d777c80](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/d777c80), [fe6deec](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/fe6deec), [604d38e](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/604d38e)).
