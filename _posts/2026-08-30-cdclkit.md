title: "cdclkit: a readable CDCL SAT solver where every answer comes with a certificate"
date: 2026-08-30
tags: [tool, rust, math]
---

[cdclkit](https://github.com/carlok/cdclkit) is a new public CDCL SAT solver,
preprocessor and encoding library, written from scratch in readable Python
with an optional Rust engine (roughly 18x faster) shipped as
[cdclkit-native](https://pypi.org/project/cdclkit-native/). Every answer comes
with a certificate: a satisfying model is re-evaluated clause by clause, and an
UNSAT answer emits a DRAT proof that an independent checker replays. It
publishes on PyPI with no third-party code, and the tutorial walks one problem
end to end, from DIMACS through the files between stages.
