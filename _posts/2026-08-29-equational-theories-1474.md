Contributed to [teorth/equational_theories](https://github.com/teorth/equational_theories)
an [issue](https://github.com/teorth/equational_theories/issues/1474) reporting
that the 1,062 Vampire-unresolved implications are not uniformly hard: at least
411 of them have finite countermodels on 9 to 32 elements. The analysis is
reproducible from parsimagma and disclosed in the issue as AI-produced under
human direction.
