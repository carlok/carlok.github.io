[djd0723](https://github.com/djd0723) starred [alien-hunt](https://github.com/carlok/alien-hunt) and [roundstorm](https://github.com/carlok/roundstorm) on 17 November — the two repositories are already the same project's two halves, which is likely why they were opened together: alien-hunt samples defects a plain model review of a repository does not report, and roundstorm makes persistent AI researchers deliberate for a fixed number of rounds. The login is not a drive-by: 119 public repositories, most of them forks of agent tooling — Claude Code hook collections, `humanlayer`, `AI-Scientist`, `feynman`, and an `openprover` billed as "Claude Code for mathematicians" — plus its own `claude_hooks_autowiki`, an auto-generated wiki of Claude Code hooks and plugins, and `riptide_model_router`, a `PreToolUse` hook that picks a model per subagent. That is exactly the reader both repos were built for, and a star is the only approval a stranger can give with no account tie to the author.
