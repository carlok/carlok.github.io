Note v1.48 of [diaz-modulus-lean](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean) is out, 55 pages, and it formalises four more results of Fischler 2001 with no hypothesis: Th. 2.1, which is Roy's Th. 4 in Fischler's form; Th. 2.3, the d × l form of Roy's 1992 Cor. 2, which Roy prints for 2 × 3 matrices only; Th. 5.3 with a vector hyperplane where Fischler's Conjecture 2 asks for an affine one; and Cor. 6.5 (1) ([28c55af](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/28c55af), [release](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/releases/tag/note-v1.48)).

Alongside them are Props. 3.1 and 3.2 and Ths. 4.1 and 4.2 in a pencil form that is not printed: algebraic matrices F₁, …, F_l take the place of the Lie algebra of the group and orbit dimensions become ranks. That this pencil form is the right reading of the printed statements on algebraic groups rests on an argument, not on a formal proof, and the release note says so; three routine facts of linear algebra over Q̄ come with them. Not formalised: Th. 5.1, Th. 5.4, Prop. 5.7, the conditional reductions Cor. 6.3 (2) and Cor. 6.5 (2), and the printed group-theoretic statements themselves. No printed statement needed correcting this time — in Th. 2.1 Fischler's pointer for the strict inequality leaves out one step, which Roy's Th. 4 supplies at every minimiser, and the result is correct as printed.

The library stands at 729 results, all on Lean's three standard axioms, with a 516-result blueprint; the same day it took a trial merge of Lean and Mathlib v4.36.0 at 729/729 with the axioms unchanged ([4e75841](https://github.com/carlok/diaz-modulus-lean/commit/4e75841)), and Chapter 14 gave up its novelty rankings altogether: two independent Claude referee agents re-examined every ranked entry against the pencil's normal form, Riemann–Roch on P¹ with Lüroth's degree formula and the critical pairs of Bachoc–Serra–Zemor, judged them all routine, and the "not-routine" labels came out — no result changed, and what remains claimed is only that each statement was not found in print. The [prove2me-logs](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs) entry for the same work records the theorems and pencil forms as they went in ([3a5632e](https://github.com/carlok/prove2me-logs/commit/3a5632e)).
