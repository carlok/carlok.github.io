The walkthrough of [staffarda](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda) — the model introduced when the repository [went public](/blog/2026/10/18/staffarda-public/) — is now published on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/FdIIaSr5uAI): 2 min 43 s, the three source photos and then one continuous 1080p take through the hall, in through the doorway and slaloming between the four columns at eye height, rendered in EEVEE by `blender/walkthrough.py` ([952162b](https://github.com/carlok/staffarda/commit/952162b)). The README links it behind a clickable thumbnail. The soundtrack is "Antiphona et Magnificat" by Schola Gregoriana (WSD SAC Ołtarzew), from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schola_Gregoriana-Antiphona_et_Magnificat.ogg) under CC BY-SA 3.0 — and because of that ShareAlike music the video as a whole is CC BY-SA 4.0, while the code stays MIT and the renders and documentation CC BY 4.0.
