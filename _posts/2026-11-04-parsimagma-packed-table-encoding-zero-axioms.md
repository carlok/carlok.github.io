---
title: "parsimagma: the packed table encoding closes over no axioms, measured through the competition's own judge"
date: 2026-11-04
tags: [lean4, math]
---

The question came out of the [axiom-origin correction](/blog/2026/09/05/parsimagma-axiom-origin/) of 5 September, and Haobo Ma and Wenlin Zhang's note of 2 November left it open: does a countermodel's table, read out of a single natural-number literal with `tbl / n ^ (x * n + y) % n`, close over fewer axioms than `finOpTable`? An experiment in [parsimagma](https://github.com/carlok/parsimagma) now measures it through the competition's own `verify_answer`, at commit `817a465` of its Stage 2 repository on Lean 4.33.1 — same goal, same tactic (`decideFin!`), same options, only the operation rewritten ([3832e42](https://github.com/carlok/parsimagma/commit/3832e42)).

Yes, and the difference is the whole distance. On the 100 countermodels already published, `finOpTable` closes over `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice` and is accepted 100 of 100 under the runner's default policy and 0 of 100 under a zero-axiom one; a list literal with `List.getD` keeps `propext`; a list literal with a hand-written lookup and the packed `Nat` literal close over nothing and pass 100 of 100 under either policy. Zhang and Ma's ledger — 4,240 `finOpTable` false certificates, byte-identical to one template and 1,494 distinct, carriers 2 to 9 — is accepted 1,494 of 1,494 packed under zero axioms, and the packed form is also faster exactly where it matters: 3.22 s median against 14.19 s at carrier 9, 5,298 s against 8,784 s of judge time in total, with the packed form slower in 225 of the 1,494 cases by at most 25% at the low carriers. A breadth-first walk over used constants (`AxiomPaths.lean`) puts all three axioms on the UTF-8 decoding proofs inside `String.toList`, plus `propext` from `List.getD` — the list was never the problem, the string was. Above carrier 9 there is no pair to compare, since `finOpTable` reads single digits; three models of Theorem F from `magma-1518-obstruction-lean` certify under plain `decide` at carrier 15, 39 and 75, the last at 10,915 bytes.

`PackedTables.lean` also states and proves the note's Propositions 1, 2 and 3 in core Lean with no axioms and no Mathlib — Proposition 3 through a stronger finite fact, that the table has no congruence with exactly two classes, obtained by enumerating all 203 partitions. `census.py` reproduces their expressibility census exactly (100 of 100 rows, 917 cells, 15,764 assignments, 93 bits, and their `checks.json` hash), with the split in the paper coming from the labels as emitted — one row, `order5_normal_0003`, moves. The limits are recorded rather than glossed: the judge ran with four `Judge*` modules on `JUDGE_LEAN_PATH` and no Mathlib, the 100 rows are 25 distinct tables, the ledger's other 12 false certificates were not re-encoded, and the axiom chains are shortest paths specific to the toolchain. Only aggregates of the ledger are republished; it sits on the `packed-footprint` branch.
