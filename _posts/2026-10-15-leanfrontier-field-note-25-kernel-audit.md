[LeanFrontier](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier)'s [Field Note 25](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/notes/field-note-25.html), *Fixed Before It Broke.*, tells the v4.35.0 upgrade as one dry run: the audit against v4.35.0-rc4 on 9 October failed on a single line of `ChungErdos` (`MemLp.indicator` now takes a `NullMeasurableSet`), a [two-version proof](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/500) was written a week early so the release could land unattended once Lean v4.35.0 and Mathlib appeared on 12–13 October, and [#503](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/503) then kept the v4.35 proof alone ([#508](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/508), [fe01993](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/commit/fe01993)).

The measurements taken for [issue #504](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/issues/504) are now a job rather than a note: `.github/workflows/kernel-audit.yml` replays the corpus, with its whole Mathlib closure, through Lean's kernel with `lake check` — monthly, and as of [#507](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/507)/[95f14c6](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/commit/95f14c6) once after each upgrade lands, on a push to `main` touching `lean-toolchain`, deliberately outside the blocking upgrade check it would more than double ([a3dfed7](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/commit/a3dfed7)). It runs the default kernel only ([#506](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/506), [a9125d3](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/commit/a9125d3)): the first run on an ubuntu-26.04 runner took two hours and 10.3 GB, while `--paranoid` took 3 h 19 min on a laptop and would exceed the six-hour job limit, so the bundled independent checkers are run by hand — and nanoda, one of them, dies with a stack overflow rather than a verdict while the other five accept the whole export. The job audits already-admitted code, so it changes no verdict and blocks nothing.

The same day produced the releases. [v0.3.0](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/releases/tag/v0.3.0) is 110 accepted modules on Lean and Mathlib v4.35.0, up from 102 in v0.2.0, and the matching data release [corpus-v2](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/releases/tag/corpus-v2) holds 110 submissions, 110 modules and 90 internal import edges — with the caveat its own note insists on, that every record predates 7 October and so carries triviality-probe outcomes from the old probe, which stated each goal without its module's namespace and context; re-reading all accepted entrypoints in their real context found no submission the corrected probe would reject.
