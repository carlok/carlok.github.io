[Field Note 26](https://carlok.github.io/LeanFrontier/notes/field-note-26.html), *The Roadmap Was Read.*, records nine days without a submission — the last seven of them after the contribution roadmap was refreshed — ended by seven arriving in thirteen hours. Two take up the roadmap's top directions: [Banks' 1992 theorem on sensitive dependence](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/510) is proved for any continuous, topologically transitive self-map of an infinite metric space with dense periodic points, with sensitivity defined explicitly because Mathlib has no such notion, and importing nothing from the corpus — so the corpus's own transitivity and dense-periodic-point results for the tent and logistic maps are one application away, still unapplied. The other closes the duplication Field Note 22 flagged on 4 October, when two submissions written two hours apart each defined the same way of peeling a Stern–Brocot path into runs and add-only review could not reconcile them: the quotients read from a path are now the run lengths of its encoding with one added to the last, and a companion proves the encoding injective and length-preserving ([#511](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/511), [#509](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/509)).

Four greenfield subjects follow, [#512–#515](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/512): binomial transforms that compose by adding their parameters and invert by negating them, the Turán-type identity behind the log-concavity of each row of Pascal's triangle, a uniform bound on when the iterates of any self-map of a finite set start repeating, and the strict log-convexity of the Catalan numbers from an exact positive-defect identity — that last one submitted by [qazW12345](https://github.com/qazW12345), machine-authored, with `C_(n+1)² < C_n C_(n+2)` for every `n` and no exceptional small indices ([#515](https://github.com/carlok/LeanFrontier/pull/515)). They are also the first submissions whose triviality probes ran after the 7 October correction: of 27 new entrypoints, 22 are stated in Mathlib's or the corpus's vocabulary and the six bounded tactics really ran on them as written, closing none, while the five that name their own definitions were reported as not stated from the baseline with Lean's reason rather than as "failed". The corpus stands at 117 modules and 93 internal import edges.
