---
title: "red-ink sprint 30: the ambience was four loops of filtered noise, so now it is a mains hum and a crowd that answers blows"
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [games]
---

The ambience in [red-ink](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink) was four looping noise sources — a low-passed room, two band-passed crowd murmurs, wind in bout 4 — with the crowd swelling on every hit, and filtered noise has no pitch or syllables, so it read as hiss. Sprint 30 replaced it: the room is a mains hum built from a fundamental and two partials, each two sine oscillators a few tenths of a hertz apart at base frequencies of 50, 41 and 56 Hz depending on the place, every bed event is now tonal, and the crowd is `crowdReact`, four to nine sawtooth voices through two vowel formants that answer a blow — 60 percent for a heavy, 30 for a dodge, 15 for a jab, 10 for a block, always for the knockout — at least 0.9 s apart, with nothing playing between blows ([4a438a4](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/4a438a4), [#29](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/pull/29)).

`tools/ambience-probe.mjs`, written first so the change could be measured, reports over 100 Hz to 8 kHz: looping buffer sources 4 → 0, spectral flatness 0.113–0.116 → 0.0005 for the bed alone and 0.398–0.424 → 0.0004–0.0013 just after a blow, energy above 1 kHz 14–17 percent → 0.5 percent, level −34 to −37 → −40.6 dBFS. The whoosh under every swing and dodge got the same treatment — unfiltered white noise became the same seeded noise swept through a resonant band-pass rising from 600 to 2600 Hz over the 160 ms — reading flatness 0.575 → 0.116 and 96 percent of its energy in 600 to 3500 Hz, with the other nine clips byte-identical ([5329566](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/5329566)).

Medals now respect difficulty and autoplay: none on Easy and none if the AI played any of the bout, the reason printed on the win card, medals earned on Hard kept in their own mask, and the save at version 4 ([93ef235](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/93ef235)). The sixth look also fixed a pad-only player losing the music after 40 s idle, made A or Start begin the game from the splash, added a 10 s loading watchdog that offers Reload, taught the crash catcher to re-arm after Keep playing, and put training on the lose card of the first two bouts once ([502ba2d](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/502ba2d), [ed9785d](https://github.com/carlok/red-ink/commit/ed9785d)); the regression stands at 77 tests.
