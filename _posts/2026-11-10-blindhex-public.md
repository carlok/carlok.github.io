title: "blindhex is public: GNSS interference analysed with the map hidden until the results were frozen"
date: 2026-11-10

---

[blindhex](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex) went public on 10 November, MIT-licensed ([78dec5d](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex/commit/78dec5d)): 1,727 daily [GPSJAM](https://gpsjam.org) maps from February 2022 to November 2026, 190,102 H3 cells of roughly 1,800 km² each, 62 million valid hexagon-days — and an answer. GPS interference reported by aircraft is more than three times as common as in 2022, and since late 2024 it has been appearing east of the Urals, where there was none before.

The point of the repository is how that answer was reached. Stage A replaces every H3 cell id with a random opaque id and keeps only an abstract neighbour graph, the key that maps ids back to places going to `data/sealed/`; stage B runs in a container with `--network=none`, a read-only mount of the anonymised data and no `h3`, `shapely` or `geopandas` installed, after a self-test that checks those isolation facts on every run; stage C re-hashes the frozen blind report and refuses to continue if the hash changed, and only then names places and draws the map ([README](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex), [frozen/BLIND_REPORT.md](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex/blob/master/frozen/BLIND_REPORT.md)).

Red hexagon-days were 0.4 % in 2022 and about 1.4 % a year since 2024, hexagons with at least one red day in a week went from about 300 to about 1,250, and the blind run found 565 persistent hexagons in 77 groups, six space-time clusters — the pre-registered maximum — and four three-week windows where many hexagons switched on together. East of the Urals the rate moves from 0.00 % in 2022–23 to 2.1 % in 2025 and 7.4 % in 2026 with coverage unchanged, one Omsk episode starting the day a drone alert was declared there; the caveats are in the report rather than the summary — the source shows degraded accuracy reported by aircraft and not jamming itself, it changed on 2026-08-27 so the confirmatory window ends before that, and the pre-registered positive control failed, since at least 14 real clusters are stronger than the injected one. The repository also carries the paper and a 64-second Short, re-voiced on 10 November into news order — finding, examples, oddity, caveat, method ([2fe3a38](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex/commit/2fe3a38), [59fec44](https://github.com/carlok/blindhex/commit/59fec44)).
