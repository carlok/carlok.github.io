---
title: "packed-table-footprint: written as one Nat literal instead of a string, an ETP countermodel's table stops closing over any axiom at all"
date: 2026-11-21
tags: [lean4, math, research]
---

[packed-table-footprint](https://github.com/carlok/packed-table-footprint) asks one narrow question about the encoding the equational-theories Stage 2 judge is fed: does a countermodel's operation table, read out of a single natural-number literal with `tbl / n ^ (x * n + y) % n`, close over fewer axioms than `finOpTable` — the string-decoding form the parsimagma certificates use? It does, and the gap is the whole list: `finOpTable` closes over `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`, the packed literal over nothing. Same goal, same tactic (`decideFin!`), same options, only the operation rewritten.

The measurement runs through the competition's own judge, `verify_answer` at commit `817a465` of `SAIRcompetition/equational-theories-lean-stage2` on Lean 4.33.1. On the 100 countermodels published in [parsimagma](https://github.com/carlok/parsimagma) — 800 judge calls across four encodings — the packed form is accepted 100/100 at the zero-axiom policy where `finOpTable` is accepted 100/100 officially and 0/100 at zero. On the ledger, the JSON under `results/` of `the-omega-institute/sair-eqt2-stage2-solver` at `4bf87e3` holds exactly 4,240 `finOpTable` false certificates; those reduce to 1,494 law-pair and table combinations, and all 1,494 pass packed at zero axioms in 2,988 judge calls, as emitted.

Where the axioms enter is traced rather than guessed: all three come from `String.toList`, which decodes UTF-8, and `List.getD` adds `propext` on its own through a catch-all match (`Nat.land` → `Nat.bitwise`, whose termination proof reaches `Nat.div_lt_self`) — so any catch-all match over an inductive pulls it in, and a lookup that spells out every constructor stays clean. The price is size, not time: the packed certificate is 404 B median against 323 B, larger on 1,223 of the 1,494, but at carrier 9 it takes 1.80 s alone against 7.64 s, and the whole ledger 5,298 s against 8,784 s at eight workers, because the string no longer has to be decoded in the elaborator and again in the kernel ([fec3f59](https://github.com/carlok/packed-table-footprint/commit/fec3f59)).

The rest of the ledger follows the same way. 471 of 472 judge calls on parsimagma's 820-pair `infinite-only.tsv` pass at the zero-axiom policy with an empty closure, the one rejection being the baseline run of `hard2_0027`, whose block `austin_f_lemmas_hand.txt` redoes by hand — and with that block, 148 pairs of `infinite-only.tsv` with hypothesis 1659, 2000, 2473 or 1167 go from accepted-with-two-axioms to accepted at zero. Counting both sides, 8,372 of the ledger's 8,378 rows pass the official policy without Mathlib, and 2,869 problems pass at zero once the `finOpTable` problems are packed and that one is redone.
