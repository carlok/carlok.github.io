[consculpta](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta) went public on 17 October: a browser-only breeder of 3D sculptures, Apache-2.0, live at [carlok.github.io/consculpta](https://carlok.github.io/consculpta/) after 41 commits and twenty-five pull requests on its first day. Each specimen is the region where a formula over 3D simplex noise rises above an iso level inside a sphere, cube or drum; the formula is a tree of noise, trigonometry and space transforms, and every offspring changes its parent's tree by tweaking a number, swapping a function, grafting a branch, wrapping a node or cutting one out, with roughly one in twelve switching to strands — thin filaments traced where the surface crosses a rotated copy of itself. Before anything is compiled the page evaluates each formula on 520 sample points to normalise it, estimate a safe raymarching step and discard the empty or the solid, which is what makes a litter of six cheap enough to breed in a click.

Most of the day's work was the interface around that engine: a genome editor with per-segment controls and gene relevance, stage overlay toggles, a wheel-zoom policy, touch tilt, off-screen pause and a compile overlay ([fe75a1a](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta/commit/fe75a1a), [89fb0d1](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta/commit/89fb0d1), [def25e4](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta/commit/def25e4)), then persistence for the litter and its history, share links, a lineage strip over the last ten parents, thumbnail previews, palette presets and a theme switch ([15b4a4d](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta/commit/15b4a4d), [fec157b](https://github.com/carlok/consculpta/commit/fec157b)). There is no backend: the shader export is a standalone Shadertoy shader with the formula compiled in, a snapshot renders a high-resolution PNG from the current camera, and the optional AI co-breeder is off by default and talks straight from the browser to whichever Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible endpoint the reader configures with their own key — held in page memory unless they tick the box that remembers it on the device.
