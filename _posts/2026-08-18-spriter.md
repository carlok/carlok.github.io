---
title: "spriter: raster images to pixel art, entirely in the browser"
date: 2026-08-18
tags: [tool, project]
---

[spriter](https://github.com/carlok/spriter) is a new browser-only tool that turns a
raster image into a pixel-art sprite. There's no upload, no build step, and no
dependencies — the whole conversion happens locally in the browser.
